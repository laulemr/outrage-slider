The Model, Live

Move the slider. Watch your world rearrange.

This interactive activity demonstrates how changing a ranking goal can change what appears in a social media feed.

The ranking goal in this activity is outrage. Increasing outrage causes the system to prioritize more emotionally charged and conflict-oriented content.

How the activity works

Users can adjust an outrage level from 0 to 10 using buttons or a slider.

At lower outrage levels, the feed prioritizes calmer content such as research, local events, culture, community information, and public policy.

At higher outrage levels, the feed prioritizes content that emphasizes controversy, backlash, conflict, anger, and public disputes.

Key takeaway

A feed does not simply display information. It ranks information according to the goals it is designed to optimize.

Changing the ranking goal changes what users are more likely to see, engage with, and believe is important.

This is not hypothetical. This is how recommendation systems shape information environments.
