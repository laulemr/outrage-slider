    Accessible text version of this activity

    This activity shows how changing one ranking goal can rearrange a feed.

    At lower outrage levels, the feed favours calmer stories about research, local updates, culture, and policy.

    At higher outrage levels, the feed prioritises conflict-heavy headlines about backlash, controversy, anger, and public disputes.

    The point: a feed does not simply show information. It ranks information according to what the system is optimising for.

    This is not hypothetical. This is the model.
